INFERENCE
- The avoidable MCP-output overhead in this run came mainly from broad default report selection and repeated diagnostics attached to narrow projection results. The avoidable interaction overhead came from one guessed exact symbol and one incorrect reader invocation.
- Mandatory MCP-first and pre/post verification rules also increased the number of calls, but those are workflow requirements, not evidence that the Contextor analysis engine itself is defective.
- No tokenizer or no-MCP control run was performed. These observations identify redundant content and calls, but do not quantify token savings or prove a general engine-level context cost.
